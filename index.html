About

From embryos to howler monkeys.

I'm a final-year Biological Sciences student at the University of Edinburgh, specialising in Development, Regeneration & Stem Cells, with a third of my degree in business and management. My work sits where wet-lab developmental biology meets the quantitative analysis of behaviour.

In 2026 I helped trace a progenitor contribution to the urogenital region in the Val Wilson Lab, then spent a field season at the Instituto de Neuro-etología in Veracruz, México, building an unsupervised learning pipeline to test for non-random structure in howler monkey behaviour. Before that I built an R Shiny app that lets labs compare RNA-seq fold changes across experiments.

I'm a native Spanish speaker who finished school in Nairobi, Kenya, and I'm now looking for research assistant roles and Master's or PhD places in developmental, behavioural and computational neuroscience.
